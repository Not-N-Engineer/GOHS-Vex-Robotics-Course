Module 3 · Lesson 3.1

What is cadding for Vex?

Cadding for Vex Robotics is actually incredibly simple. You do not have to design any parts; since all parts required are sanctioned and sold by Vex themselves (not a very good thing, since it gives them a monopoly on parts, but it makes things easier for us), all you have to do is use some cadding software, import the stl files provided by Vex, and assemble in a way that makes a robot.

Cadding is useful because it gives us a plan on what to do, blueprints for assembly, and allows us to know what parts to order and their cost.
