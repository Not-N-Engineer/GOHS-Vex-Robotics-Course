Module 0 · Lesson 0.2

How this club will work

Since the regional competitions start in August and September, we will not be able to attend them or the national competitions. Instead, for the first month or two, we will be doing this course and learning the skills required to do Vex robotics. Then, we will split into teams of 4-6 to design a robot per team.

In around the end of first semester or beginning of the second semester, when the robots are fully designed, we will make presentations about our robot designs (why we think it will win or deserves to be built) and present them as well as our engineering notebooks to several judges. These judges will decide on either one or two teams (depending on budget available) who will be able to design and build their robot. The teams that did not get chosen will then be split and distibuted among the chosen teams to build and program the robots.

If, for some reason, we are only able to send one team and have two teams with robots, we will probably host a mock competition to decide which team wins. This is most likely not going to happen.
