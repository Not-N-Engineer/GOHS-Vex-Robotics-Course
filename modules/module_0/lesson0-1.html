What is Vex Robotics?

Vex robotics is one of the largest annual robotics competitions in the United States. For this club, we will be competing in the Vex V5 competition for high school and college students. Each year, in around May, they release a new game which teams will develop a robot for over the summer.

The first, regional, Vex competitions usually start in August through Spetember, where you can qualify for the national competition. Later, state-wide competitions will be held all the way up to, in some cases, April, where you can qualify for the world-wide competition.

Vex also has a variety of awards that can be won, through winning the games, writing unique code, making an innnovative design, and taking good engineering notes. Recieving these awards allows you to qualify for a higher level competition.

Vex teams must design, build, and program their own robot all while taking notes on the design process to compete. Parts used must be bought from Vex's official website.
