Module 0 · Lesson 0.2

How this club will work

The meetings from September 1st through October 6th will be dedicated to completing this course to give you a basic understanding of how to make robots for Vex competitions. After that, we will split up into different teams of 4-5 and spend October 13th through November 10th using the knowledge we gain from this course to design and program our actual robots.

On November 10th, when the robots are fully designed, we will present presentations about our robot designs (why we think it will win or deserves to be built) and present them as well as our engineering notebooks to several judges. These judges will decide on either one or two teams (depending on budget available) who will be able to design and build their robot. The teams that did not get chosen will then be split and distibuted among the chosen teams to build and program the robots once the parts arrive from November 17th through December 15th.

Once the robots are ready, each member of the team will individually travel to the competition location. Our first competition is a regional competion hosted in LA on December 20th. More Information can be foind here: https://events.vex.com/robot-competitions/vex-robotics-competition/RE-V5RC-26-4599.html#general-info

If we qualify for the National competitions in regionals we will participate in that, and regardless of our success in regionals we should be able to participate in the state competition to qualify for worlds.

When all competitions are done, if we do not qualify for worlds or nationals, we will be hopefully either working on robots for other purposes and exploring how to build robots outside of vex or hosting an internal competition if the budget allows for it.
